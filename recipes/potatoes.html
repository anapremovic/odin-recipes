Air Fryer Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 5 Golden Potatoes
  • 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
  • Garlic Powder
  • Onion Powder
  • Smoked Paprika
  • Chilli Powder
  • Cumin
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat air fryer at 190° C (374° F) for 5 min.
  2. Dice potatoes into chunks.
  3. Toss with olive oil and all spices.
  4. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  5. Cook in air fryer for 15 min. Shake at halfway point.
