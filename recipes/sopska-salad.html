Šopska Salad

Ingredients

  • 4 Large Tomatoes
  • 1 Large Cucumber
  • 1/2 Cup Feta Cheese
  • 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Dice tomatoes into chunks.
  2. Slice cucumber into quarter-moons.
  3. Toss in bowl with olive oil.
  4. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  5. Crumble and top with feta cheese.
